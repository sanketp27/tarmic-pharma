About Tarmic Pharma Pvt. Ltd.

Branded pharmaceutical formulations made dependable for chronic and primary care.

Company Overview

Tarmic Pharma Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian pharmaceutical company dedicated to marketing high-quality branded formulations. Our therapeutic focus spans key chronic-care segments including diabetes management, cardiovascular health, neuropathy and nerve wellness, bone and joint care, gastroenterology, and specialty supplements.

We believe in transparency and dependability: each product is clearly labeled with active composition, pack size, and printed Maximum Retail Price (MRP).
